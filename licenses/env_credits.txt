Models are rescaled and assembled into houses, fields, forest clusters and a mine camp.
Terrain is the user's interpreted Yedamar heightmap. The roads, simple barrel/crate/hay
geometry and site layout are original project content generated for this prototype.
Material adjustments: foliage, bark and stone use a natural palette with nonmetal rough surfaces. Roofs use terracotta; town palette textures are embedded in their GLBs.
